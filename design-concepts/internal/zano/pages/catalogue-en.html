Description of the model

New quality of relaxation!

Contemporary street furniture can serve citizens. Thanks to urban furnitures one can spend time on a fresh air after exhausting day at the office. Who wouldn’t like to rest with family surrounded by beautiful urban architecture? The newest ZANO product its perfect for everyone, who like to have a break! We can gladly present to you Lounger duo - a product that will fits ideally in modern city centers, contemporary office spaces or shopping malls! Made of high quality materials Duo lounger represents an innovation in urban furniture. Its unusual shape encourages to rest, and just a moment of break on comfortable and stylish lounger will be nothing but joy! Duo lounger made by ZANO is quite wide, so you don’t have to worry - there is enough space for everyone! Just take a sit for a minute and forget about everything.

Modern line

Modern line presents top quality products made of stainless steel and carbon steel. They were designed especially for the modern architecture. What makes them different is that subtle aesthetics based on simple solid, without any unnecessary ornaments.

Our benches

Our offer includes wide selection of great retro and modern benches. Besides standard models we also produce benches without backrest, two-sided benches and hexagonal benches. Our projects are perfect for the streets, parks, shopping centers and offices.

Producer

As a leading manufacturer in Poland we always do our best to meet the customer’s requirements. ZANO is equally comfortable and experienced designing for different types of street furniture projects. We are passionate about creating beautiful, functional, sustainable models. This is achieved through a process of focused listening that seeks to truly understand and respond to the inherent nuances of each particular client.
